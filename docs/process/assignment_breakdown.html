What Is The Real-World Problem? (Cut the Jargon)

Why does this assignment exist in the first place?

Imagine you build an AI assistant (like an auto-scheduler bot). For it to work, your user needs to click "Connect Google Calendar" or "Connect Slack". To make that login popup appear, you (the developer) must register an "OAuth App" with Google or Slack first.

The Dev's Naive Expectation

  • • "I'll just get a Client ID & Secret in 5 minutes."
  • • "All OAuth providers work basically the same."
  • • "We can launch tomorrow!"

The Brutal Reality

  • • Google asks for a YouTube demo video, DNS proof, and $1,500 security audit.
  • • Wait time is 3 to 6 weeks with human auditors rejecting tickets.
  • • Documentation is buried across 5 obsolete wiki pages.

The Technical Root: Managed Auth vs. Custom OAuth App

Why Composio's documentation was referenced

In agent platforms like Composio, developers have two ways to connect tools:

Option A: Managed Auth Instant / 0 Setup

The platform (Composio) provides their own pre-verified OAuth app. When users click connect, it says "Composio wants access to your Google Calendar".

Pros: Zero setup time, instant development.
Cons: Shows third-party branding; enterprises often reject it.

Option B: Custom OAuth App The Hard Path

The developer creates their own app with the provider. When users click connect, it says "YourCompany Assistant wants access".

Pros: White-labeled, full enterprise trust.
Cons: The developer must navigate the maze of verification, scopes, and waiting.
